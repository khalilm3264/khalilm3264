### About me

I'm interested in the intersection of **healthcare, quantitative research, and software**. My background is in psychology and clinical mental health, and I've been exploring pharmacokinetics, experimental biology, and computational approaches to scientific questions.

I enjoy turning research ideas into useful, reproducible tools—from simulation models and visualizations to smaller creative projects.

### Projects

#### 🧪 [AyFlux — Acyclovir Pharmacokinetics Simulator](https://github.com/khalilm3264/acyclovirpksim)

An open-source, browser-based tool for exploring **acyclovir and valacyclovir pharmacokinetics**, with particular attention to kidney function and dialysis.

- IV acyclovir and oral valacyclovir modeling
- Hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, CRRT, and changing renal function
- Plasma, CSF, and exploratory CMMG simulations
- Interactive visualizations, sensitivity analyses, and reproducible exports
- Literature-based comparisons and ongoing scientific validation work

**Explore:** [ayflux.org](https://ayflux.org/) · [Repository](https://github.com/khalilm3264/acyclovirpksim)

<sub>AyFlux is intended for education and research, not patient-specific treatment decisions.</sub>
